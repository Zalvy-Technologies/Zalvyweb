# Zalvy Platform Overview & Ecosystem

Zalvy is an enterprise intelligent talent development and automation platform designed to empower future tech talent and accelerate enterprise AI adoption.

## Core Pillars

1. **Production AI Agents**: Autonomous workflow agents, code interpreters, research analysts, and customer support engines.
2. **AI Engineering Internships**: Hands-on project cohorts building real microservices and AI products.
3. **Cryptographic Credentials**: Tamper-proof SHA-256 digital certificate verification.
4. **Enterprise Talent Pipeline**: Direct matching between pre-assessed engineering talent and hiring technology companies.

## Platform Capabilities

- **Real-Time AI Copilot**: Streaming multi-agent assistant for resume analysis, mock interviews, and skill diagnostics.
- **RAG Knowledge Base**: Instant document retrieval grounding AI responses in official Zalvy documentation.
- **Enterprise Governance**: Comprehensive audit logs, RBAC matrix, rate limiting, and performance metrics.
