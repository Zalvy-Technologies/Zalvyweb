# Zalvy AI Internship Program & Accelerator

The Zalvy AI Internship Program is an intensive 8-12 week engineering accelerator where candidates build real AI applications and enterprise microservices.

## Program Highlights

- **Real Production Repositories**: Work on actual codebase featuring Next.js 16, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Docker, and Gemini/Ollama LLM integrations.
- **Dedicated Engineering Mentors**: Weekly code reviews, architecture syncs, and career coaching.
- **Cryptographic Certificate**: Earn an immutable SHA-256 certificate upon successful project delivery.
- **Recruiter Spotlight**: Top performers get featured directly in Zalvy's Enterprise Employer Portal.

## How to Apply

1. Submit your application at `/applications` or upload your resume to Zalvy Copilot.
2. Complete the automated AI Skill Diagnostic Assessment.
3. Participate in an interactive mock technical interview with Zalvy Interview Simulator.
4. Receive your cohort placement offer and onboarding schedule.
