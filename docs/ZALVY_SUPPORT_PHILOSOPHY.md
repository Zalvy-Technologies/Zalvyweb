# ZALVY SUPPORT PHILOSOPHY
Date: 2026-09-15

## Principles
1. Self-service first when appropriate
2. Human escalation when needed
3. Never trap customers in automation
4. Never hide contact options
5. Never fabricate support responses
6. Never promise response times that do not exist
7. Never expose private customer data
8. Never make customers repeat information
9. Every interaction creates product insight
10. Fix reliability at product level
